# Focus Raid: value and first public release

Owner direction: a freemium Android app with a ¥480 one-time Pro purchase, where
personal focus becomes part of a worldwide effort. This is a product target, not
a claim that a price has been configured or that global contribution is live.

## Value already implemented

- Free: a reliable focus timer, Tower and Abyss expeditions, earned companions,
  illustrated keepsakes and personal boss progress. Payment does not accelerate growth.
- Pro: Star Route, detailed statistics and access to all historical records.
  The app uses the store's localized price; configure the intended Japanese price
  in Google Play instead of hardcoding ¥480 into the purchase action.
- Every credited session can advance an ongoing personal adventure. Rag, Miko and
  Lune share the locked growth rules. The world boss is 環焔竜ヴォルガ.

## Required before promising worldwide combined focus

The present app reads shared world state and sends preset Footprints. Local focus
credit does not yet mutate authoritative world HP. Do not market that as live
aggregation or present preview participants as real users.

A separately scoped contribution integration must demonstrate:

1. Two installations complete sessions and observe the same authoritative total.
2. Retrying, reconnecting or restoring a process never counts a session twice.
3. Offline focus still completes locally and pending contribution is clearly shown.
4. The client cannot directly set global HP; server-side validation and bounded
   contribution rules protect the shared result.
5. World resets/defeats have an explicit version so old pending sessions cannot
   accidentally damage a different raid.

Keep this integration small and distinct from purchases, chat or social accounts.

## Initial product evaluation

Use a small voluntary Play test cohort before making acquisition claims. Ask
whether the first session feels rewarding, whether a second session is desirable,
whether Pro's benefits and one-time price are understood, and whether the shared
world display is understandable. Review actual purchase and restore behavior on
Play. Do not add analytics SDKs or claim retention/conversion figures without a
separate implementation and disclosure decision.

Later candidates include an opt-in shareable focus result, a clearer next-growth
preview, and seasonal visual variations. These are ideas, not shipped features
or reasons to delay fixing reliability and the shared-focus promise.
